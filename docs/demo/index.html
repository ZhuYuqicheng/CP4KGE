Conformal prediction for knowledge graph embeddings

A model ranks every answer. KGCP tells you where to stop.

A knowledge graph embedding model scores every entity as an answer to a query, but a ranking does not say how many of them to trust. KGCP turns the scores of any such model into an answer set that contains the true answer with a probability you choose.

See how it works Try it on real models

One library for three papers: answer sets (NAACL 2025), guarantees per relation (Findings of ACL 2025) and intervals for uncertain knowledge graphs (EMNLP 2025). Every number on this page comes from a published model checkpoint and is recomputed in your browser.

Paper 2 · Findings of ACL 2025 · CondKGCP

Ninety percent on average can hide relations that are covered far less

Split CP promises coverage over all queries together. Calibrating each relation on its own fixes that, but rare relations then have too few held-out queries and get every entity as their answer. CondKGCP merges rare relations into similar ones and caps the sets by rank.

Paper 3 · EMNLP 2025 · UnKGCP

For uncertain knowledge graphs, an interval around the confidence of a triple

In ConceptNet, NELL or STRING, a triple holds with a confidence between 0 and 1, and a model predicts it. Conformal prediction turns that single number into an interval. UnKGCP makes the interval as wide as the model is unsure: narrow near 0 and 1, wide near one half.
